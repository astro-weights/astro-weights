`astro-weights gate` reports the generation cap and the share of records that hit it for every arm on the confirm and test splits, in the report, in `--json` and in `verdict.json` (`max_new_tokens`, `cap_hit`). New optional `GATE.toml` key `max_cap_hit` refuses the gate (exit 2, no verdict, test split not spent) when any arm is above it; without the key nothing changes.
